Update: Location Flag & Blocker (v1.2.2)

A Quick Note from the Creator

Over the past few weeks, our community has grown past 15,000+ active users with a 4.98 rating. Thank you so much for your feedback and support on Chrome and Firefox!

Maintaining this extension takes continuous engineering time; adapting to X’s API changes, running our Cloudflare database for instant lookups, and keeping your feed fast and private. While most extensions in this space sell out, inject ads, or lock basic features behind a paywall, I will never do that.

My commitment to you: All existing features (country flags, local time, basic country blocking, and timeline detection) will remain 100% FREE forever.

To keep this project independent and fund new power-user features, I will be introducing an optional Supporter / Pro tier in an upcoming update. If this extension brings you value, even a small $5 coffee on Ko-fi helps tremendously; and anyone who supports on Ko-fi automatically gets Lifetime Pro status! Thank you so much to anyone that will support me and the project!

Block Inaccurate Region (VPN) New filter option in the popup to automatically hide or block accounts whose location is flagged by X as inaccurate, broad-region, or masked via VPN.
Bypass Followed Accounts (Most Requested!) One of your most requested features by far! Enabled by default, accounts you actively follow are now protected and will never be hidden or auto-blocked, even if they match a blocked country.
One-Click Dashboard Blocking Block or unblock countries directly from the "Top Countries" sidebar in the Feed Geography Dashboard, complete with live "Blocked" badges on the world map.
Self-Healing Country Database Upgraded our Cloudflare consensus engine and local cache to automatically evict invalid bio strings and accurately resolve 195+ countries and official regions.
Anti-Rate-Limit & Instant Lookup Engine Fixed a cache bug that skipped Cloudflare lookups on timelines, added smart request pacing to prevent X 429 rate limits, and added support for X’s latest GraphQL schema.

Thank you for helping keep this project independent and free for everyone!
